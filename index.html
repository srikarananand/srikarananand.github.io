National Champion · Purdue Data4Good Datathon

Optimizing TAPS support with automated journey mapping

With Edgar Castillo, Bennett Frohock and Josias Hernandez Perez, M.S. Business Analytics and Data Science, Oklahoma State.

Challenge
TAPS staff tracked each grieving military family's journey by hand.
Built
A language model reads emails, calls and texts. A Random Forest places each survivor in one of six phases.
Result
Staff can reach out at the right moment and spot who needs help sooner.

Honest read: 0.94 F1 is for the survey classifier. A hand-check of 30 log entries matched 19.
